Good to know

Is everything marked veg actually vegetarian?

Yes. A green dot means vegetarian and a red dot means it contains egg or meat.

Can I get a drink in a larger size?

Every coffee and cold drink has a Large option for ₹40 extra, chosen on the menu card.

How does the free charm work?

Once your cart reaches ₹500, pick one charm in the cart or the charm section. It is added at no cost.

Can I bring my own book?

Please do. Swap it for one from the Reading Room shelf, or just read quietly.
